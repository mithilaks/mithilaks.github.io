Predicting U.S. domestic flight delays

Northeastern University · with 1 teammate
  • Python
  • XGBoost
  • CatBoost
  • LightGBM
  • scikit-learn
  • Optuna
  • SHAP

Built a model to predict whether a flight departs at least 15 minutes late, using only pre-departure schedule data from 200,000 flights in the 2015 DOT/BTS dataset, and turned the results into a clear view of where an operator could focus.

  • Compared 14 model configurations, including baseline, tree-ensemble, neural network, and stacking models. The best, XGBoost tuned with Optuna, reached a holdout AUC of 0.652 and F1 of 0.37.
  • Detected a target-encoding leak that inflated cross-validation AUC by 0.085 (0.718 vs. 0.633) and rebuilt a fold-safe validation so performance estimates could be trusted.
  • Used SHAP to show that a route’s historical delay rate was the strongest predictor, and tuned the decision threshold to raise recall from 61% to 65% at unchanged F1.

Spring 2026

Behavioral and policy drivers of youth tobacco use

DS 5110, Northeastern University · with 3 teammates
  • Python
  • XGBoost
  • SHAP
  • K-Means
  • UMAP
  • statsmodels
  • pandas

Combined CDC Global Youth Tobacco Survey data (1999–2018), WHO MPOWER policy scores, and World Bank indicators into 522 country-year observations (30,535 survey records), handling 18% missing data with regional-median imputation.

  • Predicted youth smoking prevalence with XGBoost and country-grouped cross-validation. SHAP showed that susceptibility, secondhand smoke exposure, and policy strength were the main drivers.
  • Segmented countries into risk profiles with K-Means and UMAP, and ran a policy scenario analysis suggesting stronger policy in high-risk countries could lower prevalence by up to 7.34 percentage points.
  • Tested policy impact with difference-in-differences and found no statistically significant effect (p = 0.88), so the scenario results are reported as directional, not causal.
