Predicting U.S. domestic flight delays

  • Python
  • XGBoost
  • CatBoost
  • LightGBM
  • scikit-learn
  • Optuna
  • SHAP

Predicted whether a flight departs at least 15 minutes late, using only pre-departure schedule data from 200,000 flights in the 2015 DOT/BTS dataset.

  • Compared 16 model configurations across baseline, tree-ensemble, neural network, and stacking approaches. The best model, XGBoost tuned with Optuna, reached a holdout AUC of 0.65 and F1 of 0.37.
  • Found a target-encoding leak that inflated cross-validation AUC by +0.084 relative to the holdout (0.718 vs. 0.65). Rebuilt the validation so encodings are fit inside each fold; the corrected CV AUC is 0.633.
  • Used SHAP to show that a route's historical delay rate was the strongest predictor. Lowering the decision threshold to 0.48 raised recall from about 61% to about 65% with F1 essentially unchanged.

Behavioral and policy drivers of youth tobacco use

  • Python
  • pandas
  • XGBoost
  • SHAP
  • K-Means
  • UMAP
  • statsmodels

A multi-country analysis of youth smoking prevalence that combined survey, policy, and economic data into one panel.

  • Integrated CDC Global Youth Tobacco Survey records (30,535 across 187 countries, 1999–2018), WHO MPOWER policy scores, and World Bank indicators into 522 country-year observations, using region-based imputation for missing values.
  • Predicted prevalence with XGBoost using country-grouped cross-validation. SHAP identified behavioral and exposure factors, including susceptibility to future use and secondhand smoke, as the primary drivers.
  • Grouped countries into risk profiles with K-Means and UMAP. A policy scenario analysis suggested stronger policy in high-risk countries could lower prevalence by up to 7.34 percentage points.
  • A difference-in-differences test found no statistically significant policy effect (p = 0.88), so the scenario results are reported as directional, not causal.
